斯坦福神经生物学家如何平衡科学与信仰

How a Stanford neurobiologist balances science and faith

斯坦福神经生物学家如何平衡科学与信仰

原文链接: https://news.stanford.edu/stories/2018/06/neuroscientist-balances-science-faith

June 21st, 2018

William Newsome is a world-class neurobiologist and a Christian man of faith. He spoke to Stanford News about how he became a scientist and the tensions, real and imagined, between science and religion.
威廉・纽瑟姆（William Newsome）是世界级神经生物学家，同时也是一名虔诚的基督徒。他在接受《斯坦福新闻》采访时，谈及了自己成为科学家的历程，以及科学与宗教之间那些真实存在或想象中的矛盾。

To some, William Newsome might seem a tad unusual. He is not only a world-class scientist who has made extensive contributions to the study of vision and the neurobiological underpinnings of choice but is also a church-going Christian.
在有些人看来，威廉・纽瑟姆或许有些与众不同。他不仅是一位世界级科学家 —— 在视觉研究以及 “选择” 行为的神经生物学基础研究领域做出了卓越贡献 —— 同时也是一位常去教堂的基督徒。

Nathan Collins/Ian Terpin
内森・柯林斯（Nathan Collins）/ 伊恩・特平（Ian Terpin）

William Newsome talks about growing up the son of a Southern Baptist minister and how his faith and interest in science led him to study the brain.
威廉・纽瑟姆讲述了自己作为南方浸信会牧师之子的成长经历，以及信仰与科学兴趣如何促使他投身大脑研究领域。

Newsome, the Harman Family Provostial Professor and the Vincent V. C. Woo Director of the Stanford Neurosciences Institute, leads an institute devoted to understanding how the human brain gives rise to mental life and shapes who we are. He talked to Stanford News about growing up the son and grandson of Baptist ministers, the wonders contained in a single drop of water and how he bridges the worlds of science and religion.
纽瑟姆目前担任哈曼家族教务长教授（Harman Family Provostial Professor），同时也是斯坦福神经科学研究所文森特・V・C・吴主任（Vincent V. C. Woo Director）。他所领导的研究所致力于探索人类大脑如何产生精神活动，以及如何塑造个体特质。在接受《斯坦福新闻》采访时，他谈及了自己作为浸信会牧师之子与孙辈的成长背景、一滴水中蕴含的奥秘，以及他如何搭建起连接科学与宗教两个世界的桥梁。

Did you feel any pressure from your family not to pursue science growing up?
成长过程中，你是否感受到家人反对你从事科学相关事业的压力？

My father and mother were what I would call orthodox Christian believers, but they never created any barriers between my interest in science and the Christian faith that they wanted to raise me in. I remember hunting for fossils with my father when I was a kid, and my father was particularly interested in new paleontological discoveries on hominid origins, things like that. So I had a healthy interest in both science and in matters of religious faith in my background. I’ve always been grateful for that.
我的父母是传统的基督徒，但他们从未在我对科学的兴趣和他们希望我秉持的基督教信仰之间设置任何障碍。我记得小时候常和父亲一起寻找化石，他对人类起源相关的古生物学新发现这类话题格外感兴趣。因此，在成长背景的影响下，我对科学和宗教信仰都保持着健康的兴趣。我一直对此心怀感激。

Was there a moment when you knew you wanted to actually pursue a career in science?
是否有某个瞬间，让你确定自己想要以科学为职业？

I got really interested in biology early on. I had a great course from a great teacher in the ninth grade. I remember the first time I looked at an apparently, to my naked eye, clear drop of water – looked at it under the microscope and saw all these little bugs swimming and moving around in a clear drop of water. I was just enthralled by that and made up my mind right then and there I really wanted to be a biologist when I grew up.
我很早就对生物学产生了浓厚兴趣。九年级时，一位优秀的老师给我上了一门很棒的生物课。我还记得第一次观察水滴的场景：肉眼看上去清澈透明的一滴水，放在显微镜下后，我看到里面有无数小生物在游动。那一刻我完全被吸引了，当场就下定决心，长大后一定要成为一名生物学家。

What tensions do you see between science and faith?
你认为科学与信仰之间存在哪些矛盾？

There are two ways in which one could find tension. One, which I think is a red herring, is saying that the discoveries of science make religious faith untenable.
人们感知到的矛盾主要有两种。其中一种 —— 我认为是无关紧要的干扰 —— 是声称科学发现会让宗教信仰站不住脚。

I actually think that most of the discoveries of science are open to a religious worldview. I think that the discovery of the big bang, for example, shows that our universe has not been in existence forever, that there was a moment where it started, and that’s very consistent with the notion of creation in early Genesis stories.
但事实上，我认为大多数科学发现都可以与宗教世界观兼容。比如，宇宙大爆炸理论的发现表明，我们的宇宙并非永恒存在，而是有一个起源的开端，这与《创世记》早期故事中 “创造” 的概念高度吻合。

The theory of evolution has been a flash point obviously. People have said that religious faith requires purposeful creation and evolution depends on random mutations and random events, so how can anything that depends on randomness be purposeful? That’s a red herring. Scientists, including my own laboratory, use random events to purposeful ends all the time. That’s why people who create good random number generators are so valued in science.
进化论显然是一个争议焦点。有人认为，宗教信仰主张 “有目的的创造”，而进化论依赖于随机突变和随机事件，那么依赖随机性的事物怎么可能具有目的性呢？但这其实是个无关紧要的问题。科学家（包括我所在的实验室）一直都在利用随机事件来实现有目的的目标。这也是为什么能设计出优质随机数生成器的人在科学界如此受重视。

What is the real tension?
真正的矛盾是什么？

Where the real tensions exist are the habits of mind in science and the habits of mind in religion. In science, we want everything to be objective, so if I do an experiment properly in my lab it can be replicated anywhere in the world given the adequate conceptual background and technical expertise. In religion, we’re searching for meaning and trying to construct and perceive patterns of meaning in our lives. There’s some objectivity in that, but it’s much more reliant on intuition, much more reliant on gut-level feelings.
真正的矛盾在于科学与宗教所要求的思维习惯不同。在科学领域，我们追求一切事物的客观性 —— 如果我在实验室里规范地完成了一项实验，那么在具备足够理论背景和技术能力的前提下，无论在世界哪个地方，其他人都能重复出相同的实验结果。而在宗教领域，我们则在探寻意义，试图构建并感知生活中的意义模式。这其中固然存在一定客观性，但更多依赖于直觉，依赖于内心深处的感受。

You’ve said that science is good for answering some questions, while faith or something like it is good for other questions, like whether to get married.
你曾说过，科学擅长解答某些问题，而信仰之类的事物则更适合解答另一些问题，比如 “是否要结婚”。

This is a favorite example, because when you marry someone you’re really making a commitment. It’s a declaration of hope, and yet there’s no scientific experiment you can do that proves that this is the right person to marry. If you wait for scientific proof, you’ll be waiting an awfully long time.
这是我很喜欢用的一个例子，因为结婚本质上是做出一份承诺，是对未来的一种期许。但没有任何科学实验能证明 “这个人就是你合适的结婚对象”。如果你非要等科学证据出现，那恐怕要等上很久很久。

Now that doesn’t mean that you check your brain at the door. You think really hard, but in the end you don’t have proof and you have to take a step of faith.
当然，这并不意味着你要放弃理性思考。你会认真权衡，但最终你没有确凿证据，只能凭信心迈出这一步。

And yet scientists have tried to figure out what makes marriages work. Are there areas of overlap between science and religion?
不过，科学家确实在尝试研究 “婚姻幸福的秘诀”。那么科学与宗教之间是否存在重叠领域呢？

I think that there are definitely areas of overlap where you want to bring both kinds of evidence to bear. When I speak to religious communities of students at colleges or other events, I always tell them that your faith should be informed by science. It should not be replaced by science. Science can’t bear that weight. It can’t give you the things that you need from faith or well-reasoned philosophy. But your faith should always be informed by science.
我认为肯定存在重叠领域，在这些领域中，我们需要同时参考科学和宗教两方面的依据。当我在大学或其他场合与有宗教信仰的学生群体交流时，我总会告诉他们：信仰应接受科学的启发，但不应被科学取代。科学无法承担 “替代信仰” 的重任，它无法给予你从信仰或严谨哲学中所能获得的东西。但你的信仰始终应该受到科学的启发。

It sounds like in your faith community there’s a lot of room for exploring different perspectives and challenging each other. There’s something similar in how you’ve constructed the Neurosciences Institute – there’s an interest in building community and merging different viewpoints. Is there a parallel?
听起来，你所在的信仰社群有很大空间允许人们探索不同观点、相互质疑。而你构建神经科学研究所的方式似乎也有相似之处 —— 注重打造社群、融合不同视角。这两者之间是否存在关联？

You know, I like science and I like running a lab, but the things that I like most are the personal relationships of trying to figure things out in a lab.
其实，我热爱科学，也喜欢管理实验室，但我最喜欢的，是在实验室里与同事们通过协作探索问题时建立的人际关系。

Deep down, I’m finding that I am like my father in important ways. I may be a pastor at heart. Community means a lot to me, and mentoring means a lot to me, and the personal relationships within science mean a lot to me. The politics, on the other hand, I frequently find off-putting. My father really liked the personal aspects of ministry, but disliked the political aspect of church life. Amusingly, perhaps, I’m coming back around toward home after 50 years, realizing that I’m more like my father than I ever believed.
从内心深处来说，我发现自己在很多重要方面和父亲很像。或许我本质上有着 “牧师” 的特质。社群对我意义重大，指导他人对我意义重大，科学领域内的人际关系对我也意义重大。但另一方面，我常常对 “权力斗争” 感到反感。我父亲当年就很喜欢牧师工作中与人打交道的部分，却厌恶教会生活中的政治色彩。有趣的是，50 年后的今天，我似乎又回到了 “原点”，意识到自己比以往任何时候都更像父亲。
